Start each task from the screen named in **Start**. Success is judged only by the condition in **Done when**.

## Task 1 — HR: publish a job (start: signed in as the participant's HR account, Dashboard)
"Create a job called Marketing Coordinator that requires the skills excel, communication and marketing, and publish it so candidates can apply."
